## 👋🏾 About me

I’m a software engineer, founder, and curious builder interested in the space between an idea and something genuinely useful.

I completed a diploma in Software Engineering at Centennial College in Canada. After graduating, I spent more than three years freelancing and building software used in real production environments.

That experience taught me that creating good technology requires more than writing code. It requires understanding people, communicating clearly, making thoughtful tradeoffs, and remaining focused on the problem being solved.

Today, I’m applying those lessons while building **Bondtrail** and sharing what I learn about:

- Technology and software engineering
- Startups and early-stage company building
- Product and business strategy
- Leadership and responsibility
- Human-centred technology
- Building and learning in public

      <a href="https://bondtrail.app"><strong>Visit Bondtrail →</strong></a>
    </td>

Many family safety products treat continuous monitoring as the price people must pay for reassurance.

I believe there is a more respectful approach.

Bondtrail is exploring how families can check in on one another without making anyone feel constantly watched.

> Safety and autonomy do not have to be enemies.
